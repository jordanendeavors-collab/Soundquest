What is a frequency?

Sound is the air wiggling. Frequency counts how many wiggles happen in one second. That count has a name: Hertz (Hz).

Few wiggles a second = a low rumble. Lots of wiggles = a high squeak. Slide the bar and watch the wave speed up.

Double the number and you get the same note, higher — that's an octave. 220, 440, 880 are all an “A”. Try the ×2 button.

Humans hear about 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz. Dogs hear way higher. The very low 40 Hz is one you feel in your chest more than you hear.
